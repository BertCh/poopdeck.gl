# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual and
romantic identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Showing empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning when mistakes happen.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Sexualized language or imagery, unwanted sexual attention, or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, intimidation, or threats.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents
the project in public. Project spaces include the repository, issue tracker,
reviews, discussions, community events, and project-related communication.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to <rgcgeog@gmail.com> with `[conduct]` in
the subject. Include relevant links or screenshots when it is safe to do so.
Do not open a public issue about a sensitive conduct concern.

The project maintainer will review reports as confidentially and promptly as
practical, may request more information, and will communicate an outcome when
appropriate. There is no guaranteed response time or formal appeals board; this
is a maintainer-led project. Retaliation against anyone making a good-faith
report is not permitted.

Responses will be proportional to the behavior and may include a private
correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project
spaces. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments
that violate this policy.

Security vulnerabilities follow the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md),
not the conduct-reporting route above. General help belongs in the channels
described in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
